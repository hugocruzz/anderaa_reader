Step by step on how to use the water tank safely.

Prerequisites:
- MFCs ui
- Anderaa sensor ui
- A2PS Subocean soft

Steps:
Preparation:
0) Switch on a subocean and wait until it reaches 40 degrees 
1) Moisturize the oxygen sensor (Anderaa)
2) Launch the anderaas, MFCs and subocean user interfaces.
3) ON the Electrical box, switch on the system and the MFCs.
4) Put the membrane and connect the pumps, and the sensors.
5) Connect the membrane output to the water tank gas outlet.
6) Direct the Helium inflow to the water tank with the valves.
7) Open the Helium input to the MFCs. 


Security check:
[] All valves are closed.

Operation, degazing phase. 
1) Check if helium is reaching the water tank by opening the valve, then close the valve.
1) Fill the water tank with water.
2) Set the Helium flow to 1 L/min on the MFCs user interface.
3) Open the valve, you should see bubbles and inspect if no water enters the tube.
4) Set the water mix
5) close the water tank without seiling it.
5) Let it degaze until it reaches 0%. ATTENTION, DO NOT SWITCH OFF THE MFCs, CLOSE THE VALVES BEFORE.
6) Pump the water into the other pump, this will serve to increase the pressure aftwerwards. 
7) replenish the water tank if needed and redo the process.

Pressurizing phase:
1) Close the water tank, and seil it.
2) Set the desired methane concentration in the MFCs interface.
3) Wait a certain amount of time until the concentration stabilizes.
